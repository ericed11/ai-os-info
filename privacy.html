Privacy policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This policy applies to the private application associated with this information page.

Access and use

Connected-account data is accessed under permissions granted by the account holder and used to provide authorized functions. Access is limited by the permissions granted and the operation requested.

Storage and processing

Authorization credentials, selected account data, derived results, and operational records may be stored for the application’s operation. Relevant data may be shared with configured third-party processing, hosting, and backup providers. Their terms, account settings, and retention policies apply to data they process.

Connected-account data is not sold or used by this application for advertising.

Retention and control

There is no single automatic deletion period for all records. Stored records remain until removed by the operator or an applicable retention process. Backup copies may remain under the provider’s retention settings.

The account holder can revoke Google access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete previously stored records. The operator manages deletion of application-held records; provider-held copies remain subject to the relevant provider’s controls.

Public information site

These static pages do not collect form submissions or use operator-added analytics or advertising trackers. The hosting provider may collect technical request data under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
